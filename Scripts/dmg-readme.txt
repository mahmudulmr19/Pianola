PIANOLA — plays Roblox piano sheets for you

Needs a Mac with macOS 26 (Tahoe) or newer.


INSTALL

1. Drag Pianola onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Pianola from your Applications folder.
   macOS says it can't check the app for malware, because it isn't from the
   App Store. Click Done.

3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Pianola. Confirm with your password.
   (You only do this once.)

4. Pianola asks for Accessibility access, which it needs to press keys in
   Roblox. Click Open System Settings and switch Pianola on.

5. In Pianola, click Test Keys (the keyboard button at the top) to check that
   everything works.

If macOS ever says Pianola "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal and
run this line, then open Pianola again:

    xattr -cr /Applications/Pianola.app


PLAY

1. Open Roblox and sit at a piano.
2. In Pianola, click +, paste a sheet, and type the song's BPM.
3. Press the big Play button. Pianola switches to Roblox, counts 3-2-1 and plays.

Shortcuts that work inside Roblox:
   Control-Option-P   play / pause
   Control-Option-S   stop
   Control-Option-[   5 BPM slower
   Control-Option-]   5 BPM faster

Clicking away from Roblox pauses the song, so keys never go to another app.
Press Control-Option-P to carry on.
